# Market matrix: Germany, UK, Costa Rica, Mexico, Colombia

Planning defaults for B2B SMB audiences. Verify against the human's own data after the first read.
Legal notes are a checklist for the human to confirm, not legal advice. Re-check before each launch since
rules and platform policies move.

## Platform fit for SMB owners and managers

| Market | Lead platform | Second | Chat channel | Language and register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Germany | LinkedIn | Meta (FB + IG) | Email / form, rarely WhatsApp for first contact | German, **Sie**. Precise, proof-heavy, low hype. |
| UK | LinkedIn | Meta | Email / form | British English. Understated. Avoid US superlatives. |
| Costa Rica | Meta (IG + FB) | LinkedIn | **WhatsApp** | Spanish, **usted**. Warm, relationship first. |
| Mexico | Meta (FB + IG) | LinkedIn, TikTok | **WhatsApp** | Mexican Spanish, usted for B2B. Local examples (CDMX, MTY, GDL). |
| Colombia | Meta (IG + FB) | LinkedIn | **WhatsApp** | Colombian Spanish, usted. Local examples (Bogotá, Medellín, Cali). |

Implications:
- Do not run one Spanish ad for all three LatAm markets at launch. At minimum swap examples, currency
  and city references per country. One ad set per country.
- Germany and UK: Instagram alone rarely reaches the decision-maker. Pair with LinkedIn or accept that
  Meta is the cheaper pool-builder and LinkedIn is the closer.
- LatAm: click-to-WhatsApp often beats a form. Staff it or don't run it.

## Compliance checklist per market

### All markets
- Platform ad policies (Meta Advertising Standards, LinkedIn Ads Policies). No personal attribute
  targeting in copy ("Are you struggling with…?" style phrasing that asserts a personal attribute gets
  rejected on Meta; reframe as "For teams that…").
- AI-generated or AI-altered imagery, video or voice: platform AI disclosure on.
- Claims of results: substantiation on file, typical result stated if a specific result is shown.
- Testimonials: written consent, real customers, real words.

### Germany (EU)
- **DSGVO** (GDPR) plus **TDDDG** (formerly TTDSG): Meta Pixel / LinkedIn Insight Tag only after opt-in
  consent. Banner must allow "reject" as easily as "accept". Conversions API events must respect consent.
- **Impressum** on the landing page (DDG § 5) and a Datenschutzerklärung naming Meta/LinkedIn processing.
- **UWG**: no misleading claims, no fake urgency, comparative claims must be verifiable.
- Lead forms: purpose of processing stated, link to privacy policy, no pre-ticked marketing consent.
  Email marketing to leads needs separate double opt-in.
- **EU AI Act Art. 50** (from 2 Aug 2026): disclose AI-generated or manipulated image, audio or video that
  could be taken as real. Also disclose chatbots that talk to leads.

### UK
- **UK GDPR + PECR**: cookie consent for pixels, consent for marketing emails (soft opt-in limited).
- **ASA / CAP Code**: claims must be substantiated, "free" must mean free, no misleading urgency.
- Company details and privacy notice on the landing page.

### Costa Rica
- **Ley 8968** (protección de datos personales), regulator **PRODHAB**. Informed consent for collected
  lead data, stated purpose, privacy notice.
- **Ley 7472** consumer protection: no misleading advertising.

### Mexico
- **LFPDPPP** (data protection law, replaced in 2025): **aviso de privacidad** linked from the lead form
  and landing page. Verify current regulator and wording before launch.
- **PROFECO** / Ley Federal de Protección al Consumidor: no misleading advertising, prices clear.

### Colombia
- **Ley 1581 de 2012** and Decreto 1377 de 2013: prior, express, informed authorisation for data
  processing. Política de tratamiento de datos linked. Regulator **SIC**.
- **Ley 1480 de 2011** (Estatuto del Consumidor): no misleading advertising.
